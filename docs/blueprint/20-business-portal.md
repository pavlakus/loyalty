20 - Business Portal

Purpose

The Business Portal is the central management application for business owners and managers.

It enables configuration, monitoring and optimization of loyalty programs without requiring technical knowledge.

The portal is designed around business workflows rather than administration screens.

⸻

Design Principles

The Business Portal follows these principles:

* Business First
* Configuration over Development
* Simplicity
* Transparency
* Actionable Insights
* Self-Service

The portal should help businesses improve customer loyalty rather than simply manage data.

⸻

User Roles

The portal supports:

* Business Owner
* Manager
* Employee (limited)
* Future Multi-Business Administrator

Each role sees only the features required for their responsibilities.

⸻

Home Dashboard

The Dashboard answers:

* What happened today?
* How is my loyalty program performing?
* Are there any issues?
* What should I do next?

Suggested widgets:

* Today’s Visits
* Reward Points Issued
* Reward Points Redeemed
* Active Members
* New Members
* Current Campaigns
* Notifications
* Business Alerts

⸻

Business Configuration

Businesses manage:

* Company Information
* Branding
* Currency
* Time Zone
* Locations

Changes take effect immediately unless otherwise specified.

⸻

Employee Management

Businesses may:

* Invite Employees
* Assign Roles
* Assign Locations
* Disable Accounts

Permission changes are audited.

⸻

Loyalty Program Management

Businesses configure:

* Reward Rules
* Pending Period
* Point Expiration
* XP Rules
* Membership Status Levels
* Benefits

No programming knowledge is required.

⸻

Automation

The MVP provides predefined automation templates.

Examples:

* Welcome Bonus
* Birthday Bonus
* Double Points
* Happy Hour
* Spend Bonus
* Visit Challenge

Businesses enable and configure templates.

Custom automation rules are introduced in future versions.

⸻

Customer Management

Businesses may:

* Search Customers
* View Membership
* View Reward History
* View XP History
* View Status History

Businesses may never edit immutable transaction history.

Corrections are performed through approved business operations that generate compensating transactions.

Historical financial records are never edited directly.

⸻

Manual Operations

Authorized users may perform:

* Reward Point Adjustment
* XP Adjustment
* Membership Status Adjustment

Every manual action requires:

* Reason
* Operator
* Audit Record

⸻

Loyalty Network

Businesses manage:

* Network Membership
* Partner Businesses
* Redemption Permissions
* Conversion Rules
* Active Periods

Future versions may include partner discovery.

⸻

Notifications

Businesses configure:

* Transactional Templates
* Marketing Templates
* Notification Channels
* Scheduling

Businesses never access provider credentials.

⸻

Reports

Standard reports include:

* Reward Point Activity
* XP Activity
* Membership Growth
* Redemption Statistics
* Customer Activity
* Loyalty Network Activity

Reports support export.

⸻

Analytics

The portal provides key business indicators.

Examples:

* Repeat Customer Rate
* Reward Redemption Rate
* Membership Growth
* Average Spend
* Customer Retention
* Active Members

Analytics should lead to actionable business decisions.

⸻

Business Alerts

The portal proactively informs businesses.

Examples:

* Points expiring soon
* Failed notifications
* Loyalty campaign ended
* Low customer activity
* Integration issues

The portal should highlight important actions rather than expecting users to search for them.

⸻

Integrations

Businesses manage:

* API Keys
* POS Integrations
* Webhooks
* External Services

API credentials are securely generated and rotated.

⸻

Audit

The portal displays audit history for administrative actions.

Examples:

* Employee invited
* Reward Rule changed
* API Key regenerated
* Loyalty Program modified

Audit records cannot be edited.

⸻

Multi-Tenant Isolation

Businesses only access their own:

* Customers
* Loyalty Programs
* Reports
* Automations
* Employees

Platform administrators have separate management tools.

⸻

Future Features

Future versions may include:

* Visual Automation Builder
* Campaign Builder
* Customer Segmentation
* Marketplace
* AI Assistant
* Business Benchmarking
* Referral Programs

The architecture should support these features without redesign.

⸻

Success Metrics

The Business Portal is successful when:

* businesses configure loyalty programs without developer assistance
* owners understand performance at a glance
* daily operations require minimal effort
* businesses regularly optimize their loyalty strategy
* support requests decrease because configuration is intuitive

The Business Portal should function as the operational center of the business loyalty program rather than a traditional administration interface.
