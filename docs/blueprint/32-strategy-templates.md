32 - Strategy Templates

Purpose

Strategy Templates help businesses launch an effective Loyalty Program without requiring prior knowledge of loyalty mechanics.

Instead of configuring technical settings, businesses choose their business objective.

The platform recommends a complete loyalty strategy based on industry best practices.

⸻

Design Principles

Strategy Templates should be:

* Business-oriented
* Easy to understand
* Configurable
* Explainable
* Based on proven patterns

Businesses choose goals.

The platform recommends implementation.

⸻

Core Principle

Businesses should never be required to understand:

* Reward Point economics
* XP balancing
* Status thresholds
* Challenge design
* Campaign configuration

The platform translates business goals into technical configuration.

⸻

Strategy Selection

During onboarding the business selects one or more primary objectives.

Examples:

* Increase customer retention
* Increase visit frequency
* Increase average basket value
* Attract new customers
* Win back inactive customers
* Promote new products
* Increase off-peak sales

The selected objective determines the recommended Loyalty configuration.

⸻

Industry Templates

The platform provides predefined templates for common business types.

Examples:

* Coffee Shop
* Restaurant
* Bakery
* Retail Store
* Gym
* Beauty Salon
* Kids Play Center
* Service Business

Each template contains recommended defaults based on industry practices.

⸻

# Industry Defaults

Each Industry Template includes recommended defaults for:

- Reward Experience
- Reward Rules
- XP Rules
- Welcome Bonus
- Birthday Bonus
- Instant Rewards
- Challenges
- Notification Strategy

Businesses may modify every recommendation.

Strategy Wizard

The onboarding wizard collects business information.

Examples:

* Industry
* Average transaction value
* Approximate daily customers
* Preferred reward model
* Number of locations

Optional questions:

* Estimated gross margin
* Existing loyalty program
* Preferred communication channels

The wizard uses this information to recommend an initial strategy.

⸻

Recommended Configuration

The platform may recommend:

* Reward Point earning rules
* Reward redemption rules
* XP progression
* Membership Status thresholds
* Welcome Bonus
* Birthday Bonus
* Instant Rewards
* Challenge Templates
* Notification strategy

Businesses may accept or modify any recommendation.

⸻

# Recommended Reward Experience

Each Strategy Template recommends the most appropriate Reward Experience.

Examples:

Coffee Shop

Recommended:

Surprise Experience

Reason:

High visit frequency increases engagement through repeated Reward Opportunities.

---

Beauty Salon

Recommended:

Standard Experience

Reason:

Customers visit less frequently and generally prefer immediate and predictable rewards.

---

Retail

Recommended:

Standard Experience

Optional:

Surprise Experience during promotional campaigns.

Businesses remain free to choose a different Reward Experience.

Explainability

Every recommendation should include an explanation.

Example:

Recommended:

1 Reward Point for every 100 RSD spent.

Reason:

This configuration typically results in an effective customer reward value of approximately 5–7%, balancing customer motivation with long-term business sustainability.

Recommendations should always explain the business reasoning.

⸻

Simplified Loyalty Models

The platform supports simplified loyalty models for businesses that do not wish to use Reward Points.

Examples:

Buy 5 Coffees

↓

6th Coffee Free

or

Visit 10 Times

↓

Free Dessert

Internally, the platform may still use the standard Reward Engine.

The customer and business owner interact with a simplified model.

Configuration Flexibility

Recommendations are not mandatory.

Businesses may:

* Accept
* Modify
* Replace
* Create their own configuration

Templates accelerate onboarding but never restrict customization.

⸻

Continuous Improvement

Future versions may improve recommendations using:

* Anonymous platform analytics
* AI-assisted optimization
* Industry benchmarking
* Historical campaign performance

Recommendations should evolve over time while preserving business control.

⸻

AI Integration

Future AI capabilities may recommend strategy changes based on:

* Customer behavior
* Redemption rates
* Visit frequency
* Revenue trends
* Campaign performance

AI provides recommendations.

The business owner remains responsible for final decisions.

⸻

Success Metrics

Strategy Templates are successful when:

* Businesses complete onboarding quickly.
* Recommended configurations require minimal changes.
* Loyalty programs become operational within minutes.
* Businesses understand why recommendations were made.
* Customers begin earning and redeeming rewards immediately.

The objective is to reduce complexity while increasing the probability of business success.

⸻

Design Principles

Businesses should configure goals, not technical parameters.

The platform translates business objectives into an effective loyalty strategy using configurable, explainable and industry-specific recommendations.
