18 - Customer Mobile App

Purpose

The Customer Mobile App is the primary touchpoint between the customer and the Loyalty Platform.

Its goal is not only to display Reward Points, but to create a habit of regular usage and strengthen customer engagement.

The application should encourage customers to return frequently by making progress visible, rewards achievable and participation enjoyable.

⸻

Design Principles

The Customer App follows these principles:

* Mobile First
* Simplicity
* Motivation
* Transparency
* Gamification
* Fast Interaction

Customers should never need training to use the application.

⸻

Customer Journey

The customer journey consists of five phases.

1. Registration
2. Activation
3. Engagement
4. Progression
5. Retention

Each phase has different objectives.

⸻

Phase 1 - Registration

Goal:

Become a member within one minute.

Registration process:

Phone Number

↓

OTP Verification

↓

Basic Profile

↓

Welcome Bonus (optional)

↓

Membership Created

Registration should require the minimum possible information.

Email is optional.

⸻

Phase 2 - Activation

Immediately after registration the customer should understand:

* what Reward Points are
* how to earn them
* where they can be used
* current benefits

The first visit should motivate the customer to make the first purchase.

⸻

Phase 3 - Engagement

The application should encourage frequent usage.

Examples:

* progress bar
* current XP
* next Status
* active challenges
* available benefits
* recent transactions

The customer should always have a visible next goal.

⸻

Phase 4 - Progression

The customer should clearly understand:

Current Status

↓

Next Status

↓

Remaining XP

↓

Remaining Visits

↓

Estimated Progress

Progress should feel achievable.

⸻

Phase 5 - Retention

The application should encourage long-term loyalty.

Examples:

* expiring Reward Points
* birthday rewards
* exclusive offers
* personalized campaigns
* seasonal challenges

Retention should rely on value rather than spam.

⸻

Home Screen

The Home Screen is the most important screen.

It should answer immediately:

Who am I?

How many Reward Points do I have?

What is my Status?

What should I do next?

Suggested content:

* Membership Card (QR)
* Available Reward Points
* Pending Reward Points
* Current Status
* Progress to Next Status
* Active Challenge
* Latest Activity

⸻

Digital Membership Card

Every customer has a digital membership card.

The card displays:

* QR Code
* Membership Number (optional)
* Current Status
* Business Branding

The QR Code is the primary customer identifier.

The QR Code contains only the Public Membership Token.

It must never expose internal identifiers or personal information.

⸻

Reward Points

The customer should always understand:

* Available Points
* Pending Points
* Reserved Points
* Expiring Points

Transaction history should explain every movement.

⸻

XP and Status

The application should clearly separate:

Reward Points

↓

Monetary Value

and

XP

↓

Customer Loyalty

Customers should never confuse these concepts.

⸻

Benefits

The app should always display active benefits.

Examples:

* Gold Member
* 2x Birthday Points
* Extended Point Expiration
* Weekend Bonus

Customers should understand why they have each benefit.

⸻

Challenges

The MVP supports predefined challenge templates.

Examples:

* Visit 5 Times
* Spend 5000 RSD
* Visit on Monday
* Weekend Challenge

Future versions may introduce custom challenges.

⸻

Notifications

The application includes an in-app notification center.

Examples:

* Points Earned
* Status Upgraded
* Challenge Completed
* Points Expiring
* New Promotion

Notifications should lead the customer back into the application.

⸻

Loyalty Networks

If the customer participates in multiple Loyalty Programs, the application clearly separates them.

Customers can switch between programs.

For every program the app displays:

* current points
* status
* benefits
* participating businesses

⸻

Transaction History

Every transaction displays:

* Business
* Location
* Date
* Reward Points
* XP
* Transaction Type

Transactions should be easy to understand.

⸻

Customer Profile

The customer may manage:

* personal information
* notification preferences
* marketing consent
* preferred language

Sensitive account actions require OTP verification.

⸻

Performance

The application should feel instant.

Target response time:

Under 300 ms for common operations.

Offline caching may be introduced in future versions.

⸻

Accessibility

The application should support:

* Dynamic Text Size
* Screen Readers
* High Contrast
* Color-independent Status indicators

Accessibility is considered a product requirement.

⸻

Future Features

Future versions may include:

* Wallet
* Digital Coupons
* Gift Cards
* Referral Program
* Seasonal Events
* Marketplace Rewards
* AI Recommendations

These features should integrate without redesigning the customer experience.

⸻

Success Metrics

The Customer App is successful when:

* customers open it frequently
* customers understand their progress
* customers actively redeem rewards
* customers participate in challenges
* customers return to participating businesses more often

The objective is not only to display information.

The objective is to build long-term customer loyalty.
