03 - Business Rules

Purpose

This document defines the immutable business rules of the platform.

Business Rules describe how the platform behaves.

These rules are technology independent and must be respected by every system component.

No implementation may violate these rules.

⸻

General Principles

BR-001

The platform is event-driven.

Every business action begins with an event.

⸻

BR-002

Business Engines never communicate directly.

Communication is performed only through events.

⸻

BR-003

Automation Engine coordinates execution.

Business Engines never coordinate workflows.

⸻

BR-004

Every important business action must be auditable.

⸻

Customer

BR-100

A customer has exactly one platform account.

⸻

BR-101

Customer authentication is performed using phone number.

⸻

BR-102

Phone verification uses SMS OTP or Viber OTP.

⸻

BR-103

Email is optional.

⸻

BR-104

Customer may participate in unlimited Loyalty Programs.

⸻

Loyalty Programs

BR-200

Every Loyalty Program belongs to exactly one Brand.

Every Brand belongs to exactly one Business.

⸻

BR-201

Businesses may own multiple Brands.

Every Brand owns exactly one Loyalty Program.

⸻

BR-202

Locations belong to one Business.

⸻

BR-203

Locations may participate in multiple Loyalty Networks.

⸻

Reward Points

BR-300

Reward Points represent monetary value.

⸻

BR-301

Reward Points may:

* be earned
* become pending
* become available
* be reserved
* be redeemed
* expire

⸻

BR-302

Reward Points are immutable.

Current balance is calculated from transaction history.

⸻

BR-303

Reward Points are never modified directly.

Only transactions change balances.

⸻

BR-304

Pending Reward Points cannot be redeemed.

⸻

BR-305

Businesses define Pending Period.

⸻

BR-306

Businesses define Point Expiration policy.

⸻

BR-307

Businesses define Reward Point earning rules.

⸻

BR-308

Reward Points are redeemed using FIFO strategy.

Oldest available points are always redeemed first.

⸻

BR-309

Reward Point balance may never become negative.

⸻

BR-310

Receipt cancellation reverses earned Reward Points.

⸻

XP

BR-400

XP represents customer engagement.

⸻

BR-401

XP is independent from Reward Points.

⸻

BR-402

XP cannot be redeemed.

⸻

BR-403

XP never decreases because Reward Points were spent.

⸻

BR-404

Businesses define XP earning rules.

⸻

BR-405

XP is stored as immutable transactions.

⸻

Membership Status

BR-500

Status depends on XP and configured progression rules.

⸻

BR-501

Reward Point balance never affects Membership Status.

⸻

BR-502

Status benefits are configurable.

⸻

BR-503

Customers receive new benefits immediately after status change.

⸻

BR-504

Status progression requires all configured conditions.

Example:

Minimum XP

AND

Minimum Visits

⸻

BR-505

Status is evaluated automatically after every relevant event.

⸻

QR Membership

BR-600

QR contains only Public Membership Token.

⸻

BR-601

QR never contains:

* database identifiers
* phone number
* personal data

⸻

Transactions

BR-700

Employees never calculate Reward Points manually.

⸻

BR-701

Employees enter only:

* customer
* receipt amount

⸻

BR-702

Reward Engine performs all calculations.

⸻

BR-703

Businesses define earning rules.

⸻

BR-704

Businesses define redemption rules.

⸻

Shared Loyalty Network

BR-800

Reward Points always remain owned by the originating Loyalty Program.

⸻

BR-801

Shared Loyalty Network only defines where points may be redeemed.

⸻

BR-802

Shared redemption never transfers ownership of Reward Points.

⸻

BR-803

Every redemption stores:

* Origin Program
* Redeem Program

⸻

BR-804

Cross-program redemption must remain fully traceable.

⸻

Automation

BR-900

Every Automation Rule listens to one Event.

⸻

BR-901

One Event may trigger multiple Automation Rules.

⸻

BR-902

Automation Rules execute according to Priority.

⸻

BR-903

Automation execution must terminate.

Infinite execution loops are not allowed.

⸻

BR-904

The same Rule cannot execute twice for the same Event.

⸻

BR-905

Automation Rules may generate new Events.

⸻

BR-906

Execution finishes when no additional Events remain.

⸻

Security

BR-1000

Employees may access only assigned Business data.

⸻

BR-1001

Customers access only their own Memberships.

⸻

BR-1002

Platform Administrators may access all data.

⸻

BR-1003

Every business action is logged.

⸻

Notifications

BR-1100

Notifications are generated by Events.

⸻

BR-1101

Business Engines never send notifications directly.

⸻

BR-1102

Notification Engine is solely responsible for message delivery.

⸻

Analytics

BR-1200

Analytics are generated from business events.

⸻

BR-1201

Business data must remain reconstructable from transaction history.

⸻

Design Principles

BR-1300

Configuration is preferred over custom development.

⸻

BR-1301

Business Rules are implementation independent.

⸻

BR-1302

Business Rules have priority over technical implementation.

When implementation and Business Rules conflict,

Business Rules always win.
