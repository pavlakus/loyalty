21 - Analytics

Purpose

The Analytics module provides business insights that help owners understand customer behavior, measure loyalty performance and improve business decisions.

Analytics is not intended to display raw data.

Its purpose is to answer business questions.

⸻

Design Principles

Analytics follows these principles:

* Business First
* Actionable Insights
* Near Real-Time
* Explainable Metrics
* Drill Down Capability
* Privacy by Design

Every chart should answer a business question.

⸻

Dashboard Overview

The main dashboard answers:

* How is my business performing?
* Are customers returning?
* Is my loyalty program effective?
* What requires attention today?

⸻

Executive KPIs

The primary KPIs are:

* Active Members
* New Members
* Returning Customers
* Reward Points Issued
* Reward Points Redeemed
* Redemption Rate
* Repeat Visit Rate
* Average Receipt Value
* Customer Lifetime Value (future)
* Retention Rate

⸻

Membership Analytics

Businesses can monitor:

* Membership Growth
* New Registrations
* Inactive Members
* Membership Distribution
* Membership Churn

Charts include:

Daily

Weekly

Monthly

Yearly

⸻

Reward Analytics

Businesses can analyze:

* Points Earned
* Points Redeemed
* Pending Points
* Expired Points
* Manual Adjustments
* Reward Cost

Example questions:

How many points expired unused?

How many points were redeemed?

How much value was granted?

⸻

XP Analytics

Businesses can monitor:

* XP Earned
* XP Distribution
* Average XP per Customer
* Top Engaged Members
* XP Growth

XP analytics measure engagement, not spending.

⸻

Status Analytics

Businesses can view:

* Members by Status
* Status Upgrades
* Status Downgrades
* Average Time to Next Status
* Status Retention

Example:

How many customers reached Gold this month?

⸻

Customer Behavior

Businesses can analyze:

* Visit Frequency
* Average Days Between Visits
* Purchase Frequency
* Returning Customers
* First-Time Customers

The goal is to understand customer habits.

⸻

Campaign Analytics

For each campaign:

* Participants
* Conversion Rate
* Reward Cost
* Additional Revenue
* ROI (future)
* Completion Rate

Businesses should immediately see whether a campaign was successful.

⸻

Automation Analytics

Businesses can monitor:

* Automation Executions
* Trigger Frequency
* Successful Executions
* Failed Executions
* Average Execution Time

Automation should be measurable.

⸻

Notification Analytics

Businesses can analyze:

* Sent Notifications
* Delivered Notifications
* Failed Notifications
* Open Rate (Push)
* Click Rate (future)

Notification performance should be visible.

⸻

Loyalty Network Analytics

Businesses participating in a Loyalty Network can monitor:

* Cross-Business Redemptions
* Incoming Customers
* Outgoing Customers
* Estimated Settlement Value
* Partner Activity

⸻

Operational Analytics

Businesses can monitor:

* API Activity
* Integration Status
* Failed Transactions
* Queue Size
* Notification Queue
* Processing Time

Operational issues should be visible before they become business problems.

⸻

Drill Down

Every metric should support drill-down.

Example:

Reward Points Redeemed

↓

By Business

↓

By Location

↓

By Day

↓

By Customer

Users should move from overview to detail without changing modules.

⸻

Alerts and Recommendations

The Analytics module generates proactive alerts.

Examples:

* Membership growth slowing
* High number of expiring points
* Campaign underperforming
* Increased customer inactivity
* Failed integrations

Future versions may include AI-generated recommendations.

Recommendations assist business users in decision making.

Analytics never modifies business configuration automatically.

⸻

Exports

Businesses can export reports in:

* CSV
* Excel
* PDF (future)

Exports respect user permissions.

⸻

Data Freshness

Operational dashboards:

Near Real-Time

Business reports:

Within configurable reporting intervals

Analytics should balance freshness and performance.

⸻

Security

Businesses may only access analytics for their own data.

Sensitive customer information is anonymized where appropriate.

Analytics respects all tenant boundaries.

⸻

Future Extensions

Future versions may include:

* AI Insights
* Predictive Churn Analysis
* Customer Segmentation
* Revenue Forecasting
* Benchmarking
* Industry Comparison
* Cohort Analysis
* Funnel Analysis

These features should build upon the existing analytics architecture.

⸻

Success Metrics

The Analytics module is successful when business owners can answer the following questions without external tools:

* Are customers returning more often?
* Which campaigns are effective?
* Which benefits are most valuable?
* Which customers are most engaged?
* Is the loyalty program profitable?
* What should I improve next?

Analytics should guide decisions, not simply present numbers.
