# 47. Analytics Catalog

# 1. Purpose

Analytics nije samo izveštavanje.

Njegov cilj je da odgovori na pitanja:

- Da li loyalty povećava promet?
- Da li kupci dolaze češće?
- Da li se isplati davati nagrade?
- Koje kampanje rade najbolje?
- Gde gubimo kupce?
- Šta preporučiti vlasniku sledeće?

Analytics koristi podatke iz:

- Reward Engine
- XP Engine
- Status Engine
- Benefits
- Challenges
- Reward Goals
- Instant Rewards
- Notifications
- Automation Engine
- Receipt Processing

# 21. Program Health Score

Platform calculates a health score.

Range:

0–100

Components:

Retention

Repeat Visits

Reward Redemption

Inactive Members

Automation Usage

Campaign Performance

Notification Engagement

Business Growth

The Program Health Score is an analytical indicator only.

It must never directly trigger configuration changes or Business Actions without explicit Business approval.

Health Levels:

Excellent

Healthy

Needs Attention

Critical
